## 1. Document purpose and authority

### 1.1 Purpose

To state, in one place, the CIVOLINK brand identity, legal disclosure rules, approved colour palette, approved logo assets, product co-branding relationships and implementation rules — so that developers, CLI agents and designers all work from the same approved reference.

### 1.2 Authority and precedence

When documents disagree about branding, this order applies:

1. **Owner-supplied brand information** recorded in the U-001B brief (§2 below) — highest authority.
2. **The approved SVG source files** in the CIVOLINK Assets/logo folder — the approved vector artwork source (§11). The separate 2026-11-10 composite-board PNG crops are proposed raster artwork and do not supersede that approved family.
3. **This document** — the written brand reference.
4. **`docs/CIVOLINK_UMBRELLA_PRODUCTION_ARCHITECTURE.md`** — production architecture; references this document for branding.
5. **`docs/umbrella-reference/`** — a *reference design suite only*; its palette and hero copy are superseded as brand standards by this document (see §9.5, §7.4).

### 1.3 What this document does not do

It does not approve a font, does not fix clear-space or minimum-size numbers, does not publish entity status or registration date, does not authorise contact channels, domains, hosting or analytics, and does not resolve the owner decisions it does not explicitly name (§25).

## 3. Public brand versus registered business identity

Three distinct layers must never be conflated:

| Layer | Name | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Public brand** | CIVOLINK | Used in logos, navigation, headlines, product names and marketing copy. Always written `CIVOLINK` (capitals), never stylised otherwise in body copy. |
| **Registered business name** | CIVOLINK ENTERPRISES | Used where the *legal entity* must be identified: footer identity block, legal/business-information page, About page, privacy and terms pages, contract and invoice contexts. |
| **Registration number** | BN-55SLLRED | Optional public disclosure, always **together with** the registered business name (§5). |

**Distinction statement (use wherever the entity must be identified):**

> CIVOLINK is a brand operated under CIVOLINK ENTERPRISES, a registered business name in Kenya. Registration No.: BN-55SLLRED.

The exact wording may be refined for presentation, but **the legal meaning must be preserved**: a brand *operated under* a registered business name — not a company, not a corporation.

## 8. Brand values and communication principles

### 8.1 Values implied by the approved positioning

| Value | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Independent engineering** | Each product stands on its own; no inflated claims of shared infrastructure or shared capability |
| **One coherent face** | Products differ, but the umbrella brand speaks with one voice and one visual system |
| **Practical, not hype** | Promote what is built and true; never invent metrics, coverage, customers or dates |
| **Honest about status** | A product is *planned*, *in development* or *live* — and the badge must match reality |
| **Respectful of legal identity** | The registered entity is described accurately, never inflated into a company it is not |

### 8.2 Communication principles

1. **Accuracy over ambition.** Brand ambition (§6) never becomes a factual claim about registered activities or delivered services.
2. **Plain language.** Short sentences, no jargon walls, no unverifiable superlatives ("leading", "best", "fastest", "99.9%").
3. **Status honesty.** Never use "Live", "Available" or "Now serving" unless the thing is genuinely live.
4. **Identity discipline.** Brand copy says CIVOLINK; legal copy says CIVOLINK ENTERPRISES + BN-55SLLRED (§3).
5. **No invented specifics.** No invented email addresses, phone numbers, office addresses, social accounts, registration dates, directors or licences.
6. **Consistency.** The six colours and eight logo variants are the whole visual vocabulary (§9, §11).

## 10. Colour hierarchy and practical usage

### 10.1 Hierarchy

| Level | Colour | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| Dominant | Primary Navy `#102A43` | Headers, dark sections, primary buttons, logo |
| Ground | White `#FFFFFF` / Cloud `#F4F7F8` | Page background, cards, content surfaces |
| Body text | Graphite `#263238` | Paragraphs, list text, form labels |
| Accent | Primary Teal `#00A6A6` | Links, focus rings, key CTAs, logo accent |
| Secondary accent | Secondary Cyan `#18C3C8` | Charts, badges, highlights **on dark grounds only** for text uses |

### 10.2 Practical allocation (guidance, not a ratio mandate)

- **Navy + White/Cloud** should carry the majority of any layout — this is the brand's backbone.
- **Graphite** for reading text on light surfaces.
- **Teal** as the single interactive/attention accent — used sparingly so it keeps its signal.
- **Cyan** as a supporting accent, predominantly on navy/graphite grounds.

### 10.3 Neutral tints and opacity (implementation techniques)

Permitted techniques, explicitly **not** new brand colours:

- Navy at 8–12 % opacity over white for hairlines and dividers.
- Cloud as a full-bleed alternate section background.
- White at partial opacity over navy for secondary text on dark — **only where §21 contrast still passes**.
- A single desaturated neutral grey scale for disabled states and table rules, derived from Graphite, never replacing Graphite as body text.

## 14. Prohibited logo modifications

The following are prohibited without explicit owner approval:

1. **Recreating** the logo — no redrawing, tracing, re-typesetting or reconstruction.
2. **Approximating** the logo — no "close enough" re-creation in another tool.
3. **Regenerating** the logo — no AI-generated, vectorised-from-raster or auto-traced substitutes.
4. **Replacing** missing approved artwork with hand-made or generated artwork. If an approved asset is missing, report it missing (§26).
5. **Cropping** the logo or removing part of the wordmark or mark.
6. **Stretching, squashing or skewing** — any non-proportional scaling (breaks §13.1).
7. **Rotating** the logo (except pre-approved 90° uses on banners — not currently approved).
8. **Arbitrary recolouring** — only the eight approved variants may be used; do not tint, gradient or swap the navy/teal.
9. **Adding effects** — drop shadows, bevels, outlines, glows, textures or containers not approved.
10. **Creating unofficial variants** — no new colourways, no lockups, no badge versions.
11. **Combining** the logo with other marks to form a new composite without approval.
12. **Silently overwriting** an approved logo file. Any change to an approved asset requires explicit owner approval and a recorded checksum change (§24).
13. **Using the reference-suite placeholder mark** (`docs/umbrella-reference/assets/icons/civolink-mark.svg`) as if it were the approved brand mark (§11.4).

## 15. Typography guidance

> **No official corporate font has been approved.** Typography below is **implementation guidance only** until formally approved by the owner.

| Aspect | Guidance (unapproved) | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Headline face | A geometric or humanist sans-serif with strong capitals, suited to the uppercase tagline | matches the confident, technical tone |

## 21. Accessibility and colour contrast requirements

**Target: WCAG 2.2 AA.** The ratios below were computed from the six approved hex values (relative luminance per WCAG 2.x) — they are measured, not estimated.

### 21.1 Measured contrast ratios

|---|---|---|---|
| Primary Navy `#102A43` on White `#FFFFFF` | 14.64 : 1 | **PASS** | PASS |
| Primary Navy on Cloud `#F4F7F8` | 13.60 : 1 | **PASS** | PASS |

| Primary Navy on Primary Teal `#00A6A6` | 4.88 : 1 | **PASS** | PASS |

| White on Primary Navy | 14.64 : 1 | **PASS** | PASS |
| White on Graphite | 13.16 : 1 | **PASS** | PASS |
| Cloud on Primary Navy | 13.60 : 1 | **PASS** | PASS |
| Cloud on Graphite | 12.22 : 1 | **PASS** | PASS |
| Secondary Cyan on Primary Navy | 6.75 : 1 | **PASS** | PASS |
| Secondary Cyan on Graphite | 6.07 : 1 | **PASS** | PASS |
| Primary Teal on Primary Navy | 4.88 : 1 | **PASS** | PASS |
| **Primary Teal on White** | **3.00 : 1** | **FAIL** (passes large text / non-text only) | PASS |
| **Primary Teal on Graphite** | **4.39 : 1** | **FAIL** (large text only) | PASS |
| **Primary Teal on Cloud** | **2.78 : 1** | **FAIL** | **FAIL** |
| **Secondary Cyan on White** | **2.17 : 1** | **FAIL** | **FAIL** |

### 21.2 Binding colour rules derived from the measurements

1. **Body text:** Primary Navy or Graphite on White or Cloud only. All four combinations pass comfortably.
2. **Teal `#00A6A6` is not a body-text colour on light grounds.** On White it reaches only 3.00:1; on Cloud only 2.78:1. Use teal for large text (≥24 px regular / ≥18.66 px bold), icons, borders, focus indicators and non-text UI (≥3:1), or place it on navy where it reaches 4.88:1.
3. **Cyan `#18C3C8` must not be used for text on light grounds** (2.17:1). Use it on navy/graphite grounds (6.75:1 / 6.07:1), or as a non-text accent only.
4. **Never** set white or cloud text on teal or cyan grounds for normal-size copy.
5. **Focus indicators** must be visible: teal on white at 3.00:1 satisfies the 3:1 non-text requirement, but pair it with an additional navy outline for robustness.
6. **Status badges** must meet contrast in both text and background — verify each badge pairing against this table before use.
7. **Logo colours are exempt from recolouring** (§14) but the *surrounding* layout must still meet contrast.

## 24. Asset maintenance and change control

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Storage | Approved assets live in the **CIVOLINK Assets/logo folder** (paths in §11.2). They are the source of truth. |
| Checksums | SHA-256 for all eight assets is recorded in §11.2. Any future change is detectable as a checksum change. |
| No silent overwrite | Never overwrite an approved logo file. A changed checksum without an owner approval record is a defect. |
| Copying into repos | Copying approved assets into a repository is an owner-authorised action; it has **not** happened in this task (§11.4, §26). When done, record destination path + checksum and confirm it matches §11.2 exactly. |
| Verification on intake | After any copy, recompute SHA-256 and compare with §11.2. A mismatch means the file was altered in transit — reject it. |
| Dual-location drift | Two identical copies exist (§11.2). If their checksums ever diverge, stop and report; do not pick a winner. |
| Versioning | Any change to artwork requires explicit owner approval plus an updated §11.2 with the date and reason. |
| Decommissioning | Do not delete superseded assets without owner instruction; record them as superseded instead. |
