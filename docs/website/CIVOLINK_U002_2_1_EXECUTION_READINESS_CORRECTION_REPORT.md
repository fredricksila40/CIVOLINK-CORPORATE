# CIVOLINK U-002.2.1: Execution Readiness Corrections and Control Center Arrangement Review 
## 2. Mandatory working rules 
1. Inspect the current repository and documentation before modifying anything.  
2. Use the current working-tree state, not an assumed historical baseline.  
3. Preserve all pre-existing modifications, staged changes, untracked files and unrelated work.  
4. Do not discard, overwrite, reset, revert or clean up unrelated work.  
5. Do not commit, push, deploy or publish anything.  
6. Do not purchase or configure a domain, DNS service, VPS, hosting or external service.  
7. Do not introduce a CMS, shared authentication system, analytics platform or new backend.  
8. Do not modify CIVOLINK WIFI payment, webhook, router or production functionality.  
9. Do not modify the existing production Wi-Fi login page.  
10. Do not redesign, vectorize, replace or reconcile the existing logo assets. Retain the current provisional logo artwork and its documented status.  
11. Treat owner decisions as authoritative. Do not silently change a decision's status or invent approval.  
12. Distinguish confirmed facts, owner decisions, recommendations, assumptions and unresolved matters.
