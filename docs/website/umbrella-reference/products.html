Product 01

CIVOLINK WIFI

A managed Wi-Fi hotspot solution that gives operators controlled internet access, transparent customer connectivity and a clear M-Pesa payment direction.

  • Managed Wi-Fi hotspots — configurable packages, access duration and usage controls for a single hotspot deployment.
  • Customer connectivity — a captive-portal journey from connection through package selection to granted access.
  • M-Pesa payment direction — payment flow designed around M-Pesa, executed server-side by the product's own backend.
  • Controlled internet access — entitlements tied to purchased time or data rather than open connectivity.
  • Hotspot administration — subscriber, package and network oversight handled inside the product's own control panel.

Product 02

Biashara Flow

A business management platform for inventory, transactions and day-to-day operational workflows — aimed at helping small and growing businesses run with fewer spreadsheets and fewer gaps.

  • Inventory tracking — stock levels, movement and reorder visibility in one place.
  • Transaction records — sales and purchase history structured for reporting rather than retyping.
  • Operational workflows — repeatable processes for the routine work that quietly consumes owner time.
  • Enterprise-ready direction — an architecture intended to grow from a single shop toward multi-user business use.
  • Business growth — reporting and structure that make decisions easier as the business scales.
