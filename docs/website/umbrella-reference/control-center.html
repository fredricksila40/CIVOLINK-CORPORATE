Integration principles shown here

  • Discovery, not control. The umbrella links to products; it does not administer them.
  • No implied data flow. There is no shared database, message bus or synchronisation described as existing.
  • Optional integrations. Any future connection between products must be justified, scoped and reviewed separately.
  • Failure isolation. An outage in one layer must not cascade into another — this is a design intent, not a tested property.

Central account service

A possible future identity layer

A single place where a customer could hold an identity across CIVOLINK products is recorded as a possible direction — explicitly not an implemented, deployed or promised capability.

  • Not claimed today. No single sign-on, shared session or cross-product login exists in this suite or is implied by it.
  • Requires its own decision. Adoption would need a separate identity architecture review and explicit owner approval.
  • Security-first if adopted. Identity is the highest-blast-radius system a platform can own; the review bar must be the highest too.
  • Products stay independent. Any identity service would be optional — no product may depend on it to operate.
