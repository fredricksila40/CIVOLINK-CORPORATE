Independence

One front door, separate rooms

Every platform under CIVOLINK keeps its own systems: its own data, its own security boundaries, its own releases and its own operational responsibilities.

Shared branding does not create shared logins, shared databases or shared privileged access. Failure of one platform does not take another with it. The corporate website itself holds no product data and connects to no product backend.

  • Own database and backend per product
  • Own security boundaries and access control
  • Own release cycle and operational responsibility
  • No central account, no SSO, no shared sessions â€” today
  • The corporate site is static content only
